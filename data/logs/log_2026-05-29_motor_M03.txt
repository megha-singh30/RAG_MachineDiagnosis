MAINTENANCE LOG
Date: 2026-05-29
Asset: Cooling Water Motor M-03
Reported symptom: Rising high-frequency noise, no change at 1x. Occasional
rattly bearing sound.
Analysis: Envelope analysis showed energy at outer-race defect frequency
(BPFO) with rising broadband high-frequency content - early outer-race bearing
defect. No unbalance/misalignment indicators.
Action taken: Scheduled bearing replacement; corrected under-greasing found on
inspection.
Outcome: Bearing replaced before failure. Root cause: inadequate lubrication.
