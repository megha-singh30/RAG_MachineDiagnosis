MAINTENANCE LOG
Date: 2026-02-03
Asset: Induced Draft Fan M-12
Reported symptom: High horizontal vibration at running speed, ~1x dominant,
low axial. Amplitude grew after a dust-laden operating period.
Analysis: Spectrum showed a clean 1x peak, minimal 2x, low axial - consistent
with unbalance from dust buildup on the fan blades.
Action taken: Cleaned blades, then two-plane field balance. Vibration returned
to ISO zone A.
Outcome: Resolved. Recommended periodic blade cleaning.
