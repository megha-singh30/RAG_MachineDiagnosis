MAINTENANCE LOG
Date: 2026-04-17
Asset: Boiler Feed Pump P-07
Reported symptom: Elevated 2x running-speed vibration with high axial motion,
appeared after a coupling change.
Analysis: High 2x plus axial and a ~180 degree phase shift across the coupling
pointed to angular misalignment rather than unbalance.
Action taken: Laser alignment; found and corrected a soft foot and mild pipe
strain.
Outcome: Resolved. 2x and axial dropped to acceptable levels.
