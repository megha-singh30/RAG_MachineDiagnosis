FAULT: Shaft Misalignment

Vibration signature: high vibration at 1x AND 2x running speed, with 2x often
comparable to or greater than 1x. Strong AXIAL vibration is the hallmark.
Angular misalignment shows high axial 1x; parallel (offset) misalignment
shows high radial 2x. Phase across the coupling shifts about 180 degrees.

Distinguishing clue: presence of 2x (and sometimes 3x) harmonics plus high
axial vibration separates misalignment from simple unbalance.

Typical causes: poor coupling alignment, thermal growth, soft foot, pipe
strain pulling the machine out of position.

Recommended action: check and correct coupling alignment (laser alignment).
Verify soft foot and check for pipe strain before re-aligning.
