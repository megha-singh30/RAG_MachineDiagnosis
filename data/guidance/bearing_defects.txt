FAULT: Rolling-Element Bearing Defect

Vibration signature: energy at high, non-synchronous bearing defect
frequencies rather than at 1x. Outer race defects appear at BPFO, inner race
at BPFI, rolling elements at BSF, and cage at FTF. Early defects show up as
raised high-frequency broadband noise and are best seen with envelope
(demodulation) analysis.

Distinguishing clue: NON-integer multiples of running speed and rising
high-frequency noise. Unlike unbalance/misalignment, the energy is not at 1x
or its harmonics.

Typical causes: fatigue spalling, poor lubrication, contamination, electrical
fluting, improper installation.

Recommended action: trend the defect frequencies and envelope energy; plan a
bearing replacement before the defect broadens. Review lubrication practice.
