FAULT: Rotor Mass Unbalance

Vibration signature: dominant peak at 1x running speed, primarily in the
radial direction (horizontal/vertical). Amplitude is steady and increases
roughly with the square of running speed. Phase is stable and repeatable;
horizontal and vertical phase differ by about 90 degrees on the same bearing.

Distinguishing clue: energy concentrated at 1x with LOW harmonic content and
low axial vibration. If strong 2x/3x harmonics or high axial motion appear,
suspect misalignment instead.

Typical causes: material buildup or loss on the rotor, fouling, a missing
balance weight, or a lost blade/vane.

Recommended action: field balance the rotor (single- or two-plane). Inspect
for fouling or damage before balancing.
