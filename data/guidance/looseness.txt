FAULT: Mechanical Looseness

Vibration signature: many harmonics of running speed (1x, 2x, 3x, 4x...),
and often 0.5x sub-harmonics. Waveform is often truncated or erratic. Highly
directional (much worse in one direction). Phase is unstable.

Distinguishing clue: a long series of running-speed harmonics (and possible
half-order components) rather than a single clean peak. Erratic phase.

Typical causes: loose hold-down bolts, worn bearing fits, structural
looseness, cracked frames or bases.

Recommended action: inspect and torque mounting bolts, check bearing fits and
clearances, look for structural cracks.
