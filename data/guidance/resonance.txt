FAULT: Resonance / Critical Speed

Vibration signature: a large amplitude peak when running speed coincides with
a natural frequency of the machine or structure. Amplitude is very sensitive
to speed; a roughly 180-degree phase change occurs as speed passes through
the resonance. Amplitude drops sharply if speed moves away from the critical.

Distinguishing clue: amplitude depends strongly on SPEED and a phase shift
through the peak - not tied to a specific fault frequency.

Typical causes: operating near a structural or rotor natural frequency, often
after a speed change or a stiffness/mass change.

Recommended action: confirm with a coast-down/run-up test; change operating
speed if possible, or modify stiffness/mass to move the natural frequency.
